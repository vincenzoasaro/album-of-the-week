This site has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it sets no cookies of its own. Other services only come into play when you choose to use them, as explained below.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, it processes your IP address to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Music players

The players from YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and Amazon Music load only when you click to load or play them. From that moment, the service you chose may set cookies and process data about you under its own policy:

- YouTube: [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- Spotify: [Spotify privacy policy](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/)
- Apple Music: [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- Amazon Music: [Amazon privacy notice](https://www.amazon.com/privacy)

Your choice isn't remembered: on your next visit, no player loads until you click again.

## Data kept in your browser

If you take the quiz, your score is kept in your browser for the length of your visit, so it can appear on your share card. It never leaves your device and disappears when you close the tab. The share card is also created on your device; nothing is uploaded.

## Newsletter

## Shop links

Some shop links are affiliate links. When you click one, you are on the retailer's site and its own privacy and cookie policy applies. The retailer may use a cookie to record that you came from here.
